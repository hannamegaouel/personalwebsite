CRMFan SegmentationA/B Testing

Merchandising CRM Campaign

OGC Nice

At OGC Nice, I led a CRM campaign for merchandising, including the club's first structured A/B test.

The campaign was built on fan segmentation — treating different fan groups differently rather than sending the same message to everyone — and tested to learn what actually worked before scaling.

It set a template the club could reuse: segment, test, learn, and only then scale.

Customer ExperienceResearchSegmentation

Fan Experience Survey

OGC Nice

I designed and led a fan knowledge survey across a segmented supporter base, assessing the customer experience across its individual dimensions rather than reducing it to a single overall score.

The segmentation gave leadership a more useful view of the experience — showing where different fan groups were performing well and where specific needs were being missed, without allowing those differences to disappear into an average.

I then translated the findings into action. After every home game, each service area received a clear action plan based on fan feedback, creating a continuous cycle of review, improvement, and accountability.

The approach also created positive emulation across teams: sharing performance, recognising progress, and using incentives to encourage services to improve against their own benchmarks and learn from others.

The result was a shift from measuring fan satisfaction to actively managing and improving the fan experience — game by game, service by service.

PartnershipsDigitalActivation

Paris Basketball Partnership

DAZN

At DAZN, I structured and led a partnership with Paris Basketball designed to extend the brand's reach into a new fan base and convert that visibility into subscriptions.

The work centered on activation and digital: coordinating co-branded content and digital touchpoints, with subscriber acquisition mechanics built directly into the partnership.

It's a case study in building a sports partnership with commercial intent from the outset, every activation connected with a clear business metric.

PMOProductMarket Research

First Loyalty Program Launch

Retailer, Morocco

I ran PMO for the launch of a leading retailer's first loyalty program in Morocco, from initial concept through to go-to-market.

The scope spanned product specification, market research and benefits design — defining what the program needed to deliver for customers, and translating that into a specification the business could build against.

I worked with a multidisciplinary team, including designers, to bring the proposition to life. The result was a loyalty program grounded in what the market actually valued, and ready for launch.

PMODigitalFulfillment

First Digital App Launch

Retailer, Thailand

I ran PMO for a retailer's first digital app launch in Thailand, with direct responsibility for the fulfillment workstream.

That meant translating the app's commercial ambition into an operational reality: how orders would actually be picked, packed and delivered once the app went live. I turned that into clear technical requirements for the development team, and ran daily stand-ups with the developers throughout the build to track progress, resolve blockers, and keep delivery aligned with what the business needed.

A digital launch is only as strong as what happens behind it — the fulfillment model was built with the same rigor as the product itself.

PositioningB2BAdvisory

B2B Positioning & Value Proposition

StadiumGO

For StadiumGO, I reviewed and rebuilt the B2B value proposition and supporting deck used to position the business with prospective clients.

The core of the work was shifting the deck from product-centered to client-centered. Instead of opening with what StadiumGO offers, it now opens with the specific needs and challenges of each prospective client, and builds the case for StadiumGO around solving those.

That change affected how the first conversation landed. Prospects recognized their own situation in the pitch before they saw the product, which made the offer feel relevant rather than generic.

LecturingSports Media

Sports Media Module

University of Monaco

I gave a sports media module over a week at the University of Monaco, teaching students how media, sport and business intersect in practice.

The module combined frameworks with real case material, giving students a working understanding of how sports organizations manage media, partnerships and commercial pressure in parallel.

Teaching this way keeps me honest: explaining a framework clearly enough for a classroom is a good test of whether it actually holds up in practice.
