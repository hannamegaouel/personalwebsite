Merchandising CRM Campaign

OGC Nice

At OGC Nice, I led a CRM campaign for merchandising, coordinating personalized emailing campaigns and the club's first structured A/B tests — averaging a 15% click-through rate on CTAs and a 50% opening rate.

The campaign was built on fan segmentation — treating different fan groups differently rather than sending the same message to everyone — and tested to learn what actually worked before scaling.

It set a template the club could reuse: segment, test, learn, and only then scale.

Customer ExperienceResearchSegmentation

Fan Experience Improvement

OGC Nice

The ambition was to improve the fan experience holistically, across all its dimensions, rather than one touchpoint at a time. To get there, we first needed to make the current state factual — so I designed and put in place a fan knowledge survey across a segmented supporter base, assessing the experience across its individual dimensions rather than reducing it to a single overall score.

The segmentation gave leadership a more useful view of the experience — showing where different fan groups were performing well and where specific needs were being missed, without allowing those differences to disappear into an average.

I then translated the findings into action. After every home game, each service area received a clear action plan based on fan feedback, creating a continuous cycle of review, improvement, and accountability.

The approach also created positive emulation across teams: sharing performance, recognising progress, and using incentives to encourage services to improve against their own benchmarks and learn from others.

The result was a shift from measuring fan satisfaction to actively managing and improving the fan experience — game by game, service by service.

PositioningB2BAdvisory

B2B Positioning & Value Proposition

StadiumGO

For StadiumGO, I reviewed and rebuilt the B2B value proposition and supporting deck used to position the business with prospective clients.

The core of the work was shifting the deck from product-centered to client-centered. Instead of opening with what StadiumGO offers, it now opens with the specific needs and challenges of each prospective client, and builds the case for StadiumGO around solving those.

That change affected how the first conversation landed. Prospects recognized their own situation in the pitch before they saw the product, which made the offer feel relevant rather than generic.

Sports Media Module

Rights AcquisitionBusiness CaseCompetitive Positioning

Serie A & Saudi Pro League Rights

DAZN

I built the business case for DAZN's acquisition of Serie A and Saudi Pro League broadcast rights in France, working through the investment case against subscriber acquisition, retention and competitive positioning.

The acquisition became a key differentiator against a standalone Ligue 1+ offer, giving DAZN a broader and more compelling rights portfolio in the French market.

  • Prepared the business case and partnership model for content acquisition. Result: Serie A exclusive deal (2025–2026), Eredivisie exclusive deal (5-year term), Saudi Pro League paid-channel deal — this football-exclusive value proposition enabled DAZN to become the 1st platform by number of subscribers once the LFP opened Ligue 1 distribution to all channels, including its own platform, Ligue 1+.
  • Assessed 30+ sport contents (live, on-demand, external broadcasters content ingestion)

The result: DAZN became the first distributor of Ligue 1 in France.
