About Me

Building organisations
that perform.

I work with organisations and leaders to turn ambition into action. My career has taken me across Europe, Asia, the Americas, and Africa, working across sport and business with a consistent focus on strategy, leadership, and performance.

Learning across cultures and contexts is what shaped how I read people, organizations and markets. Over the past decade I've operated inside some of the most demanding environments in sport, health and technology, combining strategic thinking with operational delivery.

Strategy consulting at McKinsey, product and partnerships at Microsoft and DAZN, regulated health at Eli Lilly, and executive roles inside professional football clubs at OGC Nice and Sunderland AFC — where I have a track record of leading multidisciplinary teams, operating across both the football and business sides of the organization.

I bring both analytical rigour and the instinct of someone who doesn't just study these markets, but lives in them.

I bring most value where strategic intent has to become operational reality: setting direction, aligning functions around what actually matters, and staying accountable through to delivery. I've built 5-year strategies and cascaded them into department-level KPIs, owned product roadmaps end-to-end, and driven cross-border programs at scale.

What drives me is environments where performance and human experience genuinely intersect — where strategy has a direct line to how someone performs, recovers, or competes. I also advise executives and teams on turning ambition into structure, and I lecture in sport business, passing on frameworks tested in the field.
