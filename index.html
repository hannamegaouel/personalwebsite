A career built across

What drives me is where strategy has a direct line to how someone performs, recovers, or competes.

Basement Project — First-Team Facilities

Sunderland AFC

As part of executive operations at Sunderland AFC, I led the planning and delivery of the basement redevelopment for the club's first-team facilities.

The project translated performance, recovery and day-to-day operational needs into a redesigned physical space — coordinating football operations, technical staff and the business side of the club around a single brief.

It's a clear example of where strategy meets the ground: turning a facilities ambition into a delivered, operational reality.

5-Year Strategic Plan

OGC Nice

At OGC Nice, I built a 5-year strategic plan for the club and translated it into KPIs at department level — giving every function a clear, measurable line back to the club's long-term ambition.

The plan spanned both football and business operations, requiring alignment across technical, commercial and administrative teams.

It's the kind of work I find most valuable: setting direction that's ambitious enough to matter, and structured enough to actually deliver.
