01 — About

A career built
across cultures.

I've lived and worked across Europe, Asia, the Americas and Africa. Sport has been the constant at every step — as a practitioner, and as a professional. Learning across cultures and contexts is what shaped how I read people, organizations and markets.

Over the past decade I've operated inside some of the most demanding environments in sport, health and technology, combining strategic thinking with operational delivery: strategy consulting at McKinsey, product and partnerships at Microsoft and DAZN, regulated health at Eli Lilly, and executive roles inside professional football clubs at OGC Nice and Sunderland AFC — leading multidisciplinary teams across both the football and business sides of the organization.

What drives me is where strategy has a direct line to how someone performs, recovers, or competes. I bring both analytical rigour and the instinct of someone who doesn't just study these markets, but lives in them.
