01 — About

A career built
across cultures.

I've lived and worked across Europe, Asia, the Americas and Africa. Sport has been the constant at every step — as a practitioner, and as a professional. Learning across cultures and contexts is what shaped how I read people, organizations and markets.

Over the past decade I've operated inside some of the most demanding environments in sport, health and technology — strategy consulting, product leadership, regulated health, and executive roles inside professional football clubs. What drives me is where strategy has a direct line to how someone performs, recovers, or competes.
